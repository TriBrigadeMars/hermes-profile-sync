# Sample editorial review

## Editorial assessment

The sample needs substantive revision before it matches Ballotpedia's neutral, encyclopedic style. The most important problems are the ideological label `far-right`, the vague attribution `Many experts say`, the factive/interpretive phrasing around `noted` and `clearly`, and the firearms speech-tribe terminology. Several mechanical house-style issues are also present.

## Priority revisions

| Location | Severity | Dimension | Rule / bias category | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paragraph 1 | MUST FIX | Credibility | Right-wing, rightist, far-right | Replace the ideological label with specific, sourced information about party affiliation, voting history, or relevant issue positions. |
| Paragraph 1 | NEEDS CONTEXT | Credibility | Factive verbs | If the candidate's statement is an opinion or disputed claim, replace `noted` with `said`, `stated`, or `wrote`. |
| Paragraph 1 | NEEDS CONTEXT | Credibility | Editorializing / inappropriate descriptor | Remove `clearly` unless the sentence is rewritten to state the specific evidence rather than the writer's conclusion. |
| Paragraph 1 | NEEDS CONTEXT | Credibility | Firearms, guns / speech-tribe terminology | Replace `gun rights` with a description of the actual firearm policy unless the phrase is being directly attributed. |
| Paragraph 2 | MUST FIX | Credibility | Weasel words | Replace `Many experts say` with named, sourced attribution. Do not imply an expert consensus without identifying the evidence. |
| Paragraph 2 | SHOULD FIX | Credibility | Loaded language / editorializing | `major victory` is evaluative unless attributed. State the result or attribute the characterization to a source. |

## Suggested wording

Current: `Yesterday, the far-right candidate noted that the bi-partisan bill would clearly protect gun rights.`

Suggested structure: `On [specific date], [candidate name/affiliation] said the bipartisan bill would [specific policy effect stated by the candidate].`

The bracketed information requires source verification; it should not be invented by the editor.

## Mechanical/style notes

- Replace `Yesterday` with a specific date.
- Use `bipartisan`, not `bi-partisan`.
- Spell out `12` at the beginning of the sentence or restructure the sentence.
- Use `67%`, not `67 percent`.
- Replace `is comprised of` with `is composed of`, `is made up of`, or a correct `comprises` construction.
- Ballotpedia prefers `advisor` unless `adviser` is part of an official title.

## Editor questions

- What specific policies or voting record support the political characterization? Without that evidence, the replacement cannot be drafted accurately.
- Which experts are being referenced, and what evidence supports the statement described as a `major victory`?
