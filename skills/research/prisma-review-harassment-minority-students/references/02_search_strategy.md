## Search Development
Boolean search strings were developed using the PEO framework components:
- **P**opulation: minority students in US higher education
- **E**xposure: sexual harassment and discrimination
- **O**utcomes: academic, psychological, social, institutional outcomes

Search strings were adapted for each database's syntax and controlled vocabulary.
