# Search Strategy

## Overview
This document details the systematic search strategy for the scoping review on disability and pregnancy-related discrimination among minority-group students in U.S. higher education (2010-2026). Boolean search strings were developed using the PEO framework and adapted for each database's syntax.

**Date of Last Search**: August 2026
**Databases Searched**: 7 electronic databases + 5 gray literature sources + government sources

## 3. Government and Institutional Sources

### 3.1 Office for Civil Rights (OCR)
- Search OCR complaint resolution database for disability and pregnancy cases in postsecondary education
- Review Dear Colleague Letters and guidance documents (2010-2026)

### 3.2 Government Accountability Office (GAO)
- Search GAO Reports for: disability + higher education + discrimination
- Search for: Title IX + pregnancy + higher education

### 3.3 National Center for Education Statistics (NCES)
- IPEDS data on disability accommodations and graduation rates by race
- Beginning Postsecondary Students (BPS) longitudinal survey data

### 3.4 National Council on Disability (NCD)
- Reports on disability rights in education (2010-2026)

### 3.5 National Women's Law Center
- Title IX complaint data and reports on pregnancy discrimination in education
