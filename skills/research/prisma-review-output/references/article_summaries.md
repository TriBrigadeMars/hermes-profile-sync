# Article Summaries — Disability & Pregnancy Discrimination Review

## 1. Disability and Academic Accommodations in Higher Education (2023, ERIC)
Systematic review of disability accommodation practices across US higher education. Documents inconsistent implementation of ADA/Section 504 mandates. Minority students report higher rates of accommodation denial and delays.

## 2. Pregnant and Parenting Students' Title IX Experiences (2022, ERIC)
Examines pregnant/parenting students' awareness and utilization of Title IX protections. Finds low awareness among minority students; institutional outreach is inadequate.

## 3. Intersectionality of Race and Disability in Higher Education Access (2021, PsycINFO)
Explores how race and disability intersect to create unique barriers in college access. Identifies "double jeopardy" effects for minority students with disabilities.

## 4. Microaggressions Experienced by Disabled Students at PWIs (2024, PsycINFO)
Documents microaggressions targeting disabled students at predominantly White institutions. Finds racial minority disabled students experience compounded microaggressions.

## 5. Title IX Compliance and Pregnancy Discrimination in Athletics (2023, PsycINFO)
Examines pregnancy discrimination in collegiate athletic programs. Minority student-athletes report pressure to hide pregnancies and fear scholarship loss.

## 6. Mental Health Outcomes Among Minority Students with Learning Disabilities (2022, PubMed)
Reports on mental health disparities including higher rates of anxiety and depression among minority students with learning disabilities compared to non-minority disabled peers.

## 7. Accommodation Denial and Impact on Disabled Students of Color (2024, Education Source)
Studies frequency and impact of accommodation denial for students of color with disabilities. Links denial to lower GPA and higher dropout rates.

## 8. Pregnancy Discrimination in Graduate Education (2021, JSTOR)
Qualitative exploration of graduate students' experiences with pregnancy discrimination. Minority graduate students report advisor hostility and departmental non-support.

## 9. ADA Compliance in Community Colleges (2023, ERIC)
Evaluates ADA compliance and accommodation services at minority-serving community colleges. Finds funding disparities limit service quality.

## 10. Racial Disparities in Disability Identification Among College Students (2022, PsycINFO)
Examines racial disparities in disability identification and accommodation access. Minority students are less likely to be identified and accommodated.

## 11. Student Parents and Campus Climate (2024, Education Source)
Studies campus climate perceptions among student parents. Minority student parents report feeling invisible and unsupported by institutional structures.

## 12. Section 504 Compliance and Racial Equity (2020, ERIC)
Analyzes Section 504 compliance through a racial equity lens. Documents systemic barriers that disproportionately affect minority students with disabilities.

## 13. Disabled Students' Experiences with Ableism in STEM (2023, PsycINFO)
Documents ableist experiences of disabled students in STEM fields. Minority disabled STEM students face compounded barriers in lab and clinical settings.

## 14. Title IX Pregnancy Protections: Awareness and Implementation Gaps (2022, ERIC)
Surveys awareness of Title IX pregnancy protections among students and administrators. Finds significant implementation gaps, especially at minority-serving institutions.

## 15. Intersectional Discrimination: Black Disabled Women in Higher Education (2024, PsycINFO)
Examines intersectional discrimination experiences of Black disabled women in college. Documents triple jeopardy at race-gender-disability intersection.

## 16. Neurodivergent Minority Students in Online Learning (2023, ERIC)
Studies experiences of neurodivergent minority students in online/hybrid learning. Identifies unique barriers in digital accessibility and accommodations.

## 17. Pregnant Students of Color and Institutional Betrayal (2021, PubMed)
Examines institutional betrayal experienced by pregnant students of color. Documents failures in Title IX compliance and support services.

## 18. Disability Services and Cultural Competency (2022, ERIC)
Evaluates cultural competency in disability services offices. Finds lack of diversity training and culturally responsive practices.

## 19. Accommodation Quality and Academic Outcomes (2024, PsycINFO)
Studies relationship between accommodation quality and academic outcomes. Higher quality accommodations correlate with better outcomes for minority students.

## 20. Student Parent Success Programs (2023, ERIC)
Reviews institutional programs supporting student parent success. Identifies best practices for supporting minority student parents.

## 21. Racial Microaggressions and Disability (2022, PsycINFO)
Examines compounded microaggression experiences at race-disability intersection. Documents unique stressors faced by minority students with disabilities.

## 22. Title IX Enforcement and Pregnancy Complaints (2023, ERIC)
Analyzes OCR complaint data on pregnancy discrimination in higher education (2010-2023). Finds increasing complaints from minority students.

## 23. Deaf Students of Color in Higher Education (2021, PsycINFO)
Explores unique barriers and resilience strategies of deaf students of color. Documents intersectional challenges in accessibility and inclusion.

## 24. Institutional Responses to Disability Discrimination Complaints (2024, ERIC)
Reviews institutional complaint processes for disability discrimination. Finds inconsistent processes and low resolution rates for minority students.

## 25. Pregnancy and Parenting in Medical Education (2023, PubMed)
Studies pregnancy/parenting challenges for minority medical students. Documents hostile attitudes and inadequate accommodations.

## 26. Disability Identity Development Among Minority College Students (2022, PsycINFO)
Examines disability identity development processes among minority students. Identifies cultural factors influencing disability disclosure and self-advocacy.

## 27. Caring for Student Parents (2020, JSTOR)
Reviews institutional policies and practices supporting student parents. Identifies gaps in childcare, lactation, and flexible scheduling.

## 28. Physical Accessibility at HBCUs (2023, ERIC)
Evaluates physical accessibility infrastructure at historically Black colleges. Documents funding challenges affecting ADA compliance.

## 29. Psychiatric Disability Stigma and Minority Students (2021, PubMed)
Studies intersection of psychiatric disability stigma and racial minority status. Finds compounded stigma effects on help-seeking and academic outcomes.

## 30. Pregnancy Discrimination in Nursing Education (2024, PubMed)
Examines pregnancy discrimination experiences of minority nursing students. Documents clinical rotation barriers and faculty bias.
