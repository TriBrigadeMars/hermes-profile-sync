# Unified PRISMA Scoping Review Collection

## Overview
This folder contains three complete PRISMA-ScR compliant scoping reviews on discrimination against minority-group students in U.S. higher education.

## Reviews Included

### Review 1: Original — Protected Class Discrimination
**Topic:** Impact of Protected Class Discrimination on Minority-Group Students in the United States
**Location:** `review-1-protected-class/`
**Articles:** 42 studies
**Status:** ✅ Complete (all 11 files)

### Review 2: Sexual Harassment & Discrimination (2010–2026)
**Topic:** Impact of Sexual Harassment and Discrimination on Minority-Group Students Across US Colleges and Universities
**Location:** `review-2-sexual-harassment/`
**Articles:** 45 studies
**Status:** ✅ Complete (all 12 files)

### Review 3: Disability & Pregnancy Discrimination (2010–2026)
**Topic:** Impact of Disability and Pregnancy-Related Discrimination on Minority-Group Students Across US Colleges and Universities
**Location:** `review-3-disability-pregnancy/`
**Articles:** 30 studies
**Status:** ✅ Complete (all 12 files)
