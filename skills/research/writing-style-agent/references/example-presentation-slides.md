## Slide 6 (Interactive exercise)
Part I: Establishing Respect
1. A respectful behavior that you believe CU Anschutz expects from its staff and rewards.
2. A respectful behavior that you prioritize in your individual career.
3. A behavior you believe shows you respect.
One behavior does not need to support the other two, your personal answer can even conflict with your other responses.

## Slide 11 (Key question)
When Can Inappropriate Behavior Become Unlawful?
Being an unpleasant, even rude, individual is not illegal.
However, unpleasant or rude behavior can become against current civil rights law.
There are four distinct characteristics that even one may violate University Policy and the law. What are they?

## Slide 24 (Practical strategies)
Practical Strategies to Create Healthy Organizational Structure and Governance
- Invest in Lower-Risk, Higher-Agency activities to build employee trust (20/80 rule)
- Shared governance demonstrates a true mastery over organizational leadership
- Start small, start tangible, ask for external help

## Slide 25 (Reflection prompt)
What is A Change You Hope To Take Away or See from This Training?
Is it mindset-related? Something between yourself and staff you oversee?
How can staff and others see this change?
(Suggestion, not a request: A smaller, tangible change that demonstrates the correct principles of good organizational leadership is better than a theoretical half-baked change that accomplishes nothing.)
