I pride myself on my ability to translate complex policies into accessible content while maintaining the utmost confidentiality and professionalism. I currently meet this in my current role at the University of Colorado Anschutz transforming policy requirements in Title VI and IX law into training programs designed for our diverse clinical population.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my administrative expertise and passion for public health can support HARP's vital work.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
