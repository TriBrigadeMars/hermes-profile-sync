# Report Contract

A market analysis should contain, when data permits:

1. **Target definition** — title, location, industry, time window.
2. **Evidence inventory** — sources, dates, sample sizes, evidence classes.
3. **Occupation mapping** — O*NET-SOC code/title and mapping caveat.
4. **Demand skills** — prevalence, 95% interval, target-vs-comparison lift, trend.
5. **Skill bundles** — common co-occurring pairs or clusters.
6. **Experience benchmarks** — requested years and education mentions.
7. **Wage/employment benchmarks** — if OEWS data is loaded.
8. **Observed outcome associations** — only when a valid outcome corpus exists and thresholds pass.
9. **Candidate comparison** — EMPHASIZE, SECONDARY, GAP / DO NOT CLAIM.
10. **Resume implications** — evidence-backed ordering and terminology, never invented claims.
11. **Limitations** — selection bias, missingness, sample size, source scope, causal warning.

Never output a single opaque "hireability score" or a numeric prediction that a named individual will get hired.
