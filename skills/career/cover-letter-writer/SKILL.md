# Cover Letter Writer

Write a concise cover letter that connects verified candidate evidence to the target role.

Read:
- `../_career-writing-shared/references/evidence-policy.md`
- `../_career-writing-shared/references/cover-letter-principles.md`

## Procedure

1. Identify the role's two or three most important needs.
2. Select candidate evidence that demonstrates those needs or the closest truthful adjacent experience.
3. Open with specific relevance, not generic excitement.
4. Use one or two concrete examples rather than repeating the resume.
5. If explaining a transition, connect transferable evidence without claiming equivalence that is not supported.
6. Mention organization-specific facts only when supplied or reliably available in the task context.
7. Avoid unsupported claims of passion, cultural fit, mission alignment, or product familiarity.
8. Keep the close brief and professional.

## Gap handling

If a requested qualification is missing:
- do not claim it;
- optionally emphasize adjacent evidence;
- do not apologize excessively;
- if the gap is material, flag it separately for the user.

## Completion criteria

Every substantive candidate claim can be traced to supplied evidence. The letter should add narrative value beyond the resume rather than paraphrasing it line by line.
