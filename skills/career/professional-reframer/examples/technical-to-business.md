# Example: Technical to Business

## Source
"I wrote a Python script that combined four CSV reports so our team didn't have to copy the numbers by hand every Monday."

## Professional reframe
"Automated a recurring weekly reporting workflow in Python by consolidating four source files, replacing manual data compilation for the team."

## Could strengthen if confirmed
- How much time did the manual process take?
- How many people used the output?
- Did automation reduce errors or accelerate a decision/reporting deadline?

## Not assumed
No time savings, error reduction, or organization-wide impact is claimed without confirmation.
