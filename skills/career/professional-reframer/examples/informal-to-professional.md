# Example: Informal to Professional

## Source
"I was the person people came to when they couldn't figure out our database. I fixed issues and showed new employees how to use it."

## Evidence
- recurring point of contact for database questions;
- troubleshooting;
- new-employee instruction.

## Professional reframe
"Served as a go-to resource for database support, resolving user issues and training new employees on day-to-day system use."

## Deliberately avoided
- database administrator;
- enterprise system owner;
- change-management lead;
- system strategy.

Those claims require additional evidence.
