# LinkedIn Experience

LinkedIn Experience should not automatically be a pasted resume. It can provide enough context to make the user's work understandable as a professional story.

## Micro-case-study model

When evidence exists, structure a role around:

1. **Role purpose** - what the person was there to accomplish.
2. **Scope/context** - team, program, systems, audience, geography, volume, or responsibility.
3. **Challenge or need** - only when relevant.
4. **Response** - what the person did.
5. **Selected impact** - concrete result or purpose.
6. **Optional learning/transition** - only when it adds professional meaning.

Do not force all six elements into every role.

## Detail allocation

Give more space to:
- recent roles;
- roles relevant to the current target;
- roles containing differentiating proof.

Compress or omit low-relevance detail rather than making every position equally long.

## Style

- concise paragraph + bullets is often effective;
- use first person sparingly if it improves the narrative;
- avoid repetitive `Responsible for...` openings;
- prefer actions + context + business purpose;
- preserve official titles unless the user asks for explanatory parentheticals.
