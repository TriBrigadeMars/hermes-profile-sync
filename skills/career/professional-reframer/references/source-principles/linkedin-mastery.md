# Source Principles: 60 Days to LinkedIn Mastery

Source: *60 Days to LinkedIn Mastery: The Entrepreneur, Executive, and Employee's Guide to Optimize Your Profile*.

This file contains distilled operational principles, not reproduced book text.

Applied principles:
- begin profile work with a clear purpose and audience;
- Experience can provide more context than a resume by describing role purpose, challenges, responses, achievements, and learning;
- headline should communicate what the person actually does, not only the official title;
- the opening of About needs to earn continued attention;
- About benefits from a personable professional story and audience relevance;
- concrete accomplishments are stronger than self-praise;
- relevant keywords improve discoverability but should be purposeful;
- remove buzzwords and generic claims;
- prioritize recent and strategically relevant experience.

Application to professional reframing:
- LinkedIn mode is audience- and profile-section-aware;
- headline/About/Experience use different writing structures;
- the skill should show professional value instead of merely declaring it.
