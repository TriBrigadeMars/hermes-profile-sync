# LinkedIn Headline

The headline should quickly explain professional relevance rather than merely display a title.

## Useful ingredients

Use 1-3 of these, depending on evidence and target:
- role/function;
- specialty/domain;
- who/what the person supports;
- credible outcome/value;
- target-relevant search terms.

## Patterns

Function-led:
`Program Operations | Process Improvement | Cross-Functional Implementation`

Value-led:
`Helping teams turn complex policy into workable operations | Program Management`

Specialty-led:
`Data Governance | Reporting Operations | Process Improvement`

Do not force pipes or formulaic formatting if a natural headline works better.

## Avoid

- strings of flattering adjectives;
- unsupported executive identity;
- every keyword from a job description;
- vague titles with no function when the user wants discoverability;
- promises of results the evidence cannot support.

Front-load the most important professional meaning because LinkedIn surfaces truncated versions in some contexts.
