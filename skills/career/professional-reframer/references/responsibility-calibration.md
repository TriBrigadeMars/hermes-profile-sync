# Responsibility Calibration

Professional language often carries hidden assumptions about authority. Calibrate verbs to evidence.

## Participation

Evidence patterns:
- attended;
- helped;
- assisted;
- contributed;
- completed assigned work;
- provided information.

Appropriate verbs may include:
- supported;
- contributed to;
- assisted with;
- prepared;
- provided;
- participated in.

Do not infer ownership.

## Independent execution

Evidence patterns:
- performed a function independently;
- resolved issues directly;
- created recurring outputs;
- served as a go-to resource;
- coordinated routine work.

Appropriate verbs may include:
- delivered;
- resolved;
- coordinated;
- maintained;
- produced;
- administered (only when the function fits);
- trained.

## Ownership

Requires evidence that the person was accountable for the workstream, process, deliverable, system, or decision.

Possible verbs:
- owned;
- managed;
- led;
- oversaw;
- directed.

Do not infer ownership from competence or visibility.

## Strategic responsibility

Requires evidence such as:
- set objectives or direction;
- made material trade-offs;
- chose priorities;
- designed policy/roadmap;
- controlled meaningful resources;
- advised decision-makers with recognized influence;
- held accountability for organizational outcomes.

Possible language:
- developed strategy;
- set direction;
- advised leadership;
- established governance;
- shaped roadmap.

Execution of an existing strategy is not strategy creation.

## Executive-level framing

Use only where evidence demonstrates executive scope, such as organization-wide accountability, material resource authority, C-suite/board interaction, enterprise risk/strategy, or senior leadership responsibility.
