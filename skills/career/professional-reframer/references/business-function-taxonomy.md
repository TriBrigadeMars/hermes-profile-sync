# Business Function Taxonomy

Use this as a semantic map, not a phrase bank. A label is appropriate only when the evidence demonstrates the underlying function.

## Operations
- process improvement
- workflow coordination
- operational support
- service delivery
- quality assurance
- capacity/resource coordination
- standard operating procedures
- issue escalation

## People and enablement
- onboarding
- training
- coaching
- knowledge transfer
- performance support
- team coordination
- workforce planning

## Stakeholders and communication
- cross-functional collaboration
- requirements gathering
- stakeholder communication
- client communication
- vendor coordination
- executive communication
- expectation alignment

### Stakeholder management requires more than talking to people
Strong evidence can include recurring relationship ownership, conflicting-priority management, expectation setting, decision coordination, or communication of consequential changes.

## Projects and programs
- project coordination
- project management
- program management
- implementation
- dependency management
- risk management
- delivery planning
- resource coordination
- change enablement

Project/program management should not be inferred merely from participating in a project.

## Data and analytics
- reporting
- data analysis
- data quality
- performance measurement
- decision support
- dashboarding
- business intelligence
- forecasting

Do not infer analysis from data entry or reporting alone.

## Systems and technology
- system support
- user support
- technical troubleshooting
- implementation support
- configuration
- systems administration
- user enablement
- adoption support
- integration support

Do not infer administrator/engineer ownership from ordinary user support.

## Governance, risk, and compliance
- policy implementation
- compliance support
- control execution
- documentation
- audit support
- risk identification
- risk mitigation
- governance support

Do not infer governance ownership from following a policy.

## Finance and commercial
- budgeting support
- financial reporting
- forecasting support
- cost analysis
- revenue operations
- procurement
- contract support

Do not infer budget ownership, P&L responsibility, revenue generation, or savings without evidence.

## Customer/service
- customer support
- service recovery
- case management
- account support
- customer education
- issue resolution

Do not upgrade service delivery into account management unless relationship/portfolio responsibility is established.
