# Anti-Corporate-Sludge Pass

Professional language should convey function, scope, and value. Status-signaling language without information should be removed.

## Common low-information phrases

Review or remove unless the surrounding evidence makes them concrete:
- results-driven professional
- dynamic leader
- visionary
- thought leader
- proven track record
- passionate self-starter
- strategic thinker
- transformational leader
- innovative change agent
- world-class
- best-in-class
- synergize / synergies
- leverage (when `use` is clearer)
- optimize (when no specific improvement is described)
- drive (when responsibility or causation is unclear)
- spearhead (when leadership is unclear)
- robust (when it means only `large` or `complete`)
- holistic (when the scope is unspecified)

## Legitimate corporate concepts

Do not remove a term merely because it appears in business writing. Terms such as these can be precise when supported:
- stakeholder management
- process improvement
- change management
- program management
- governance
- risk management
- implementation
- operational excellence
- business intelligence
- workforce planning

The test is: does the phrase name a real function demonstrated by the evidence?

## De-sludge example

Sludge:
"Leveraged cross-functional stakeholder collaboration to facilitate enterprise-level process optimization initiatives."

Clearer:
"Coordinated with teams across the organization to implement new workflows and improve operations."

Even the clearer version still requires evidence for organization-wide scope and process improvement.
