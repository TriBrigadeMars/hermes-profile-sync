# Professional Storytelling

Storytelling is a tool for making professional evidence memorable, not for fictionalizing it.

## Use stories when they reveal

- a challenge and response;
- a meaningful professional choice;
- a recurring type of problem the person solves;
- a transition into a field or specialty;
- a concrete moment that demonstrates values or expertise.

## Keep stories useful

A professional story should have a clear connection to the target reader. Favor:
- specific situation;
- concrete action;
- consequence;
- professional meaning.

Do not add theatrical detail, invented dialogue, emotional claims, or artificial adversity.

## Conversational authenticity

Use language the user could plausibly say in a thoughtful professional conversation. Preserve technical specificity when it establishes credibility, but explain or simplify insider terminology for outsiders.
