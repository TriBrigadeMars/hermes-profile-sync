# LinkedIn About

## Goal

Create a human professional introduction that makes the user's through-line, value, and areas of expertise easy to understand.

## Default voice

Usually first person. Conversational but professional. Avoid both resume-summary stiffness and personal-branding hype.

## Suggested architecture

1. **Opening:** the professional through-line or problem the person tends to solve.
2. **Proof:** 1-3 concrete examples of scope, functions, or outcomes.
3. **Specialties:** a selective, readable group of supported capabilities/keywords.
4. **Direction:** current focus, target problems, or type of work sought when relevant.

## Opening test

The first few lines should tell a target reader why continuing is worthwhile. Do not spend them on greetings, chronology, or generic identity statements.

## Story use

A brief story is useful when it reveals professional identity, a recurring problem the person solves, or a meaningful transition. Do not manufacture vulnerability or drama.
