# Brevity and Clarity

Derived editorial principles for this skill:

- Put the audience first.
- Surface the most useful idea early.
- Prefer active verbs where they preserve meaning.
- Prefer concrete nouns over abstract status language.
- Use short, direct sentences where practical.
- Remove throat-clearing, repeated context, and filler.
- Preserve nuance that changes the meaning.
- Short is not a goal when shortening would make the claim less accurate.
- Avoid business jargon when plain professional language is clearer.
- Write like a capable human professional, not a corporate press release.

## LinkedIn scanning

For LinkedIn:
- front-load the most relevant idea;
- keep paragraphs short;
- use selective bullets when they improve scanning;
- do not repeat the same value proposition in every section;
- reserve detail for the roles/accomplishments that matter most.
