# Output Contracts

## Resume review/tailoring report

Use concise categories rather than an opaque score:

- `SUPPORTED — EMPHASIZE`
- `SUPPORTED — SECONDARY`
- `PARTIAL / ADJACENT`
- `GAP / DO NOT CLAIM`
- `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`
- `PARSING RISK`

## Claim traceability

For internal reasoning or user-requested audit:

| Claim | Evidence ID(s) | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

A claim with no evidence ID should not survive the final application-material pass.

## Cover letter review

Check:
- candidate claims supported;
- employer facts supplied/verified;
- not duplicative of resume;
- opening specific rather than generic;
- no invented enthusiasm or cultural-fit claim;
- gap language accurate and non-defensive.
