# Career Market Intelligence Integration

If the separate `career-market-intelligence` skill/report is available, use it as an upstream strategy source.

Recognized labels:

- `DEMAND`: observed requirement prevalence or occupational demand.
- `OUTCOME-ASSOCIATED`: observational association with hiring outcomes in a valid dataset.
- `CAUSAL`: only when credible causal evidence exists.
- `CANDIDATE-EVIDENCED`: candidate-supported qualification.
- `GAP / DO NOT CLAIM`: market demand without candidate evidence.

Rules:

1. Market evidence can affect ordering, emphasis, and development recommendations.
2. Only candidate evidence can support affirmative candidate claims.
3. A high-demand unsupported skill remains `GAP / DO NOT CLAIM`.
4. Do not convert market prevalence into a personal hiring probability.
5. Do not report a combined opaque "market fit" or "hireability" score.
