# Cover Letter Writing Principles

## Purpose

A cover letter should explain why this candidate is relevant to this role using evidence that complements rather than duplicates the resume.

## Recommended structure

1. Opening: target role and one specific reason the candidate is relevant.
2. Evidence paragraph: one or two strong examples tied to the role's most important needs.
3. Fit paragraph: connect adjacent experience, domain knowledge, or motivation to the organization/role without flattery or invented company facts.
4. Close: concise interest in next steps.

## Rules

- Do not open with generic enthusiasm alone.
- Do not repeat the resume line by line.
- Do not invent familiarity with the organization, products, culture, or mission.
- Do not claim passion, lifelong interest, or personal connection unless supplied by the user.
- Do not overstate a gap. If the candidate lacks a requested skill, emphasize adjacent evidence rather than pretending equivalence.
- Keep the tone professional, specific, and human.
- Prefer examples and outcomes over adjectives.
